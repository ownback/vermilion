## The 5 things a Mac user would notice most

1. **Apps crash in the driver.** Music twice today (plugin readback), Maps 5 times on 2026-11-23 ("Metal returned null
   texture"), Safari's GPU process on WebGPU render bundles (6 reports). Rows 7, 8, 15.
2. **A GPU problem ends the session.** No hang recovery or GPU restart: one stuck shader (clpeak, 2026-11-23) or a
   lost GPU (today 07:55) crashes WindowServer and the machine needs a power-off. Row 6.
3. **Games and 3D are far slower than the card.** The composite and app drawables live in system memory (Horizon ~15x
   below Linux), the working set is 6 GiB on a 24 GiB card, VRAM BOs only for two processes. Rows 3, 5, 17.
4. **WebGPU is half broken** in Safari (20 of 43 samples), and the failures take down WebGL and video in every tab.
   Row 15.
5. **The GPU looks wrong in the system.** Activity Monitor shows no GPU activity or per-app GPU time; apps show
   "... (RADV NAVI31) (NVMTL over ...)"; OpenGL apps started at login get the software renderer; the Dock, Spotlight
   and Photos analysis get no GPU. Rows 2, 4, 9, 10.

## Hard-rule risks spotted

- **Rule 1 (hardware safety):** GPU hang recovery (row 6) means queue removal / ring reset messages. They must copy what
  amdgpu sends on Navi 31 and go through `hw-safety-reviewer`. Today's 07:55 loss is the known AppleGFXHDA hot reset
  (already a hard rule), not a new risk; it shows the cost: one hot reset left the machine unusable until the 17:27 cold
  restart.
- **Rule 3 (detect, don't overwrite):** constants reported to RADV as hardware facts (`gb_addr_config 0x545`,
  GC 11.0.0, the 6 GiB / 2 GiB / 256 MiB pools), the canned RADV profile with the XT's profile for any revision other
  than 0xc8, the profile-name marketing string. Row 19, row 3.
- **Rule 6 (no Apple code or disassembly):** `src/vermilion-accel/accel/re/IOGraphicsAccelerator2.h:18` quotes an
  instruction from Apple's binary (reworded 2026-11-25, `2026-11-25-release-readiness.md`), and the reconstructed class layouts plus raw field offsets
  (`accel/re/`, `accel/iofam/`, `nvaccel_stubs.h`) come from Apple's kext. Inherited from NullMoth and needed to link;
  worth a review of what may stay.
- **Rule 8 (say what is verified):** PROJECT_KNOWLEDGE's DRM row says the Music crash is fixed (plugin `33b9a186`,
  "plays consistently"); two crashes today on `344c621f` contradict it.
- **VermilionTrust:** a kernel write into AMFI's ops vector with a weak layout check (row 21). Safe on 25G241 as
  verified; any kernel change is a panic risk until TODO 09's check exists (ROADMAP rule 8 keeps macOS pinned).
